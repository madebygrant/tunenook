## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: genuinely authored, not a category skin. The lamp discipline ("one amber mark = where you are") is a product argument, live-verified on every view; the two-scene brief produces real decisions like the `▶ {song} · TuneNook` document.title for the terminal desk. What slips is discipline leakage, not genericness: a drag shadow in a no-shadow system, 40px touch buttons against a 44px promise, dead Podnook CSS, menu-order drift.

**Deterministic scan**: CLI detector exited 0 with one advisory — `design-system-color` at src/styles/app.css:1845 (`rgb(0 0 0 / 0.4)`, the drag shadow). The in-browser overlay found 2 issues on Songs (clean on Albums/Playlists/Queue/Artists): `text-subtle #8a847a` at **3.9:1** on the page-header count and **4.3:1** on row durations — both under AA's 4.5:1, arithmetic-verified. This is where the detector caught what the design review missed: A praised the accessibility work; B measured the app's most-scanned text (durations, counts) failing AA on the composites it actually renders on. DESIGN.md's "4.9:1 on surface" holds on the pure token, not in practice. No false positives — all findings triaged real.

**Visual overlays**: yes — a **[Human]** Chromium tab was left open (`[Human] TuneNook — Songs`) with rendered labels highlighting the low-contrast spans. Limitation: AddMusic wasn't overlay-scannable with a seeded library; 5 of 6 views covered.

## Overall Impression

An unusually disciplined little player — the skeleton (IA, dock, row, sheet), the copy, and the failure handling are right for a bedside radio. The single biggest opportunity: make the measured reality match the documented promises. The spec says 44px, WCAG, no shadows, lamp-only emphasis; the rendering says 40px, 3.9:1, one shadow. Nothing wrong is structural; everything is small and fixable.

## What's Working

1. **Lamp discipline as a system, not a color choice** — current-row wash + 2px bar + glyph, progress fill, one primary fill, focus ring, live-verified on every view. This is what 1am scanability looks like.
2. **Consequence copy at destructive moments** — "The songs stay in your library", Unlink states exactly which songs leave, removal copy distinguishes copied vs linked songs. Best-in-class for a small app.
3. **Failure-tolerant playback** — skip-after-failure with a cap, folder-access re-grant inline in the dock, "Tap play to start" for blocked autoplay, space pre-check with "Add what fits". Errors are one-tap recoveries, not dead ends.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Blank first paint with no loading signal** — src/App.tsx:262 (`!loaded ? null`)
   Why: opening a multi-GB library over slow IndexedDB is a black screen; on the phone scene this is the first impression of every cold start, and the user can't tell "opening" from "broken".
   Fix: render the shell (topbar/sidebar + one `text-subtle` line, "Opening your library…") while `loaded` is false.
   Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

2. **[P1] `text-subtle` fails AA where it actually renders** — src/styles/app.css:13; detector-verified 3.9:1 and 4.3:1 vs required 4.5:1
   Why: durations and counts are the app's most-scanned values — mono-for-values is the design's own argument, and that's the text failing; DESIGN.md's contrast table is stale against rendered composites.
   Fix: lighten `--text-subtle` until it clears 4.5:1 on every surface it sits on (or move `.count`/`.song-dur` to `--text-muted`), and update DESIGN.md with measured numbers.
   Suggested command: `/impeccable polish`

3. **[P1] Touch text buttons are 40px, not the promised 44px** — src/styles/app.css:1653 (`@media (pointer: coarse)` gives `.xh-btn` 40px while icon buttons get 44px; `.xh-play` is 40px too) vs DESIGN.md "40px (44px on touch)"
   Why: the primary scene is one thumb in a dim room; Play, Shuffle, Add songs and Undo sit below the platform minimum the spec itself commits to, and the 36/40/44 mix is internally inconsistent.
   Fix: `min-height: 44px` for all `.xh-btn` variants and `.xh-play` on coarse pointers; keep 36px small buttons on fine-pointer desktop.
   Suggested command: `/impeccable adapt`

4. **[P2] Song menu is an 8-item ungrouped wall** — src/components/SongMenu.tsx:67 (live-verified)
   Why: it exceeds working-memory limits at the app's most-used context menu, and the danger action (Remove from library) sits undifferentiated one thumb-tap from "Play next".
   Fix: hairline gaps between play / add-to / navigate / destructive groups; removals stay last in `danger` text.
   Suggested command: `/impeccable distill`

5. **[P2] No path from the dock to the current song's row**
   Why: in a 600-song library the amber "where you are" mark is invisible unless you're already scrolled there; finding your place becomes recall (sort by Recently played, search) — the app's own design argument inverted.
   Fix: in the expanded player sheet, make the song title jump to its containing view, scrolled to and briefly pinning the current row.
   Suggested command: `/impeccable shape`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power user at the desk)**: the keyboard layer (Space/N/P/S/R/M/L, arrows, `/`, `?`) and shift-range select are excellent — but there's no keyboard path to Like or Add to queue from a row (open the 8-item menu and arrow through it); browser find-in-page silently dies past 200 windowed rows with no notice; no jump-to-current-song.

**Sam (screen reader / keyboard)**: the aria work is top-shelf (labelled icon buttons, dialog focus traps, aria-posinset on windowed rows, grip reorder via arrows) — but the detector measured what Sam's walk couldn't: durations and counts at 3.9–4.3:1; play/pause state changes aren't announced through `announce()`; the focus hop to each view's `h1` is visually suppressed (src/styles/app.css:158); search filtering gives no live result count.

**Casey (one thumb, dim room)**: dock in the thumb zone, persists across views, queue survives reload, sort collapses into a sheet — all right. But the sleep timer, the bedside scene's signature feature, is 3 taps deep (dock → sheet → options) every night; 40px text buttons (issue 3); search sits at the top of a 600-row list.

**Riley (stress tester)**: long titles, empties, renames and re-picked imports all behave. But reload mid-import — or an update-reload, which defers only to playback (src/App.tsx:146) — loses import progress silently; the all-failing library cascade lands 4 songs ahead on a track the user never chose, with no explanation of the skips.

## Minor Observations

- Drag-row shadow breaks "no shadows" (src/styles/app.css:1845) — the one finding all three sources agree on (design review, detector CLI advisory, DESIGN.md §6). Replace with `surface-hover` + the existing inset lamp bar, or a 1px `border-strong` outline.
- ~120 lines of dead Podnook CSS (`.xh-ep`, `.status-strip`, `.lamp-text`, `.xh-readout` — src/styles/app.css:665-925, 2116).
- Song-menu order deviates from DESIGN.md §4 (Like / Edit details inserted before Go to album / artist).
- Queue "Clear" is the only removal with no toast or undo.
- Sleep timer offers 15/30/45/60 + end-of-song, no custom value.
- `document.title` "▶ " prefix while playing — a lovely terminal-desk detail; keep it.

## Questions to Consider

1. The dock's "now playing" is the app's one always-visible lamp — should tapping it take you to *that song in the library*, not just the sheet?
2. Is 44px real? If yes, why do icon buttons get it and text buttons don't; if no, why does DESIGN.md promise it?
3. Would the phone be calmer with "Go to album / artist" as links on the row itself, leaving the sheet for actions?
4. If the whole product lives and dies in IndexedDB, does the first-open black screen deserve the same design care as the empty state?
